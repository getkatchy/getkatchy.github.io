FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Message us on LinkedIn ↗.

When can I use Katchy?

We're testing it with students now. Waitlist members get early access first, and we'll email you as soon as your spot is ready. We'd rather ship it right than promise a date.

How much does it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free. We'll be clear about pricing before anyone is asked to pay anything.

Which devices does it work on?

Katchy is being built for iPhone, iPad and Windows.

Which languages?

Lectures in Italian and English.

Does Katchy watch my face or guess my emotions?

No. Katchy never infers emotions. It reacts to things you do, like tapping the button, and any optional features stay on your device and are off unless you turn them on.

Is it allowed to record lectures?

It depends on your university and your professor. Katchy is a personal study tool. Check your course's rules, and ask the professor if you're not sure.

What happens to my email?

We only use it to tell you about Katchy's launch and early access. We don't sell it or share it for marketing. Details are in the privacy notice.

How do I leave the waitlist?

Reply “stop” to any email from us and we'll remove you.

Sounds good?

It takes ten seconds.
