How it works

Built for the moment you lose the thread.

Everyone zones out in a long lecture. Katchy is built around that moment, so you can get back in without missing what comes next.

1. The two‑minute recap

While you're in class, Katchy turns the lecture into text right on your device. When you tap “I zoned out”, it pulls out the key points from roughly the last two minutes and shows them in a few short lines. You read it in seconds and you're back.

2. One question, right away

Under the recap there's one quick question, like filling a gap in a sentence the lecturer just said. Answering takes a few seconds, and pulling something back from memory is what makes it stick (here's the research).

3. Honest check‑in

After the question, you can mark how you feel about it: Got it, Shaky or Still lost. Shaky spots come back later as quick review cards, so the gaps get filled before the exam, not during it.

4. Your notes, linked to the lecture

Take handwritten or typed notes next to the lecture. Katchy can point out parts of the lecture that aren't in your notes yet, and you can jump back to what was said when you wrote something.

Where it runs

Katchy is being built for iPhone, iPad and Windows, for lectures in Italian and English. Waitlist members get early access first.

Recording rules differ between universities and professors. Katchy is for your own study, so check your course's rules and ask your professor when in doubt.
