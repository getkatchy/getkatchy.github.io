About

Made by students who zone out too.

Katchy started with a question we kept asking ourselves halfway through 90‑minute lectures: wait, what did I just miss?

Who we are

We're a team of four university students in Milan building Katchy as part of TEF Ignition 2026. We build it for students like us first: people who want to keep up in class, not catch up the night before.

What we believe

  • Zoning out is normal. The fix is getting back in fast, not feeling guilty.
  • You should own your data. Speech-to-text on your device, no emotion guessing, nothing sent to your university.
  • Built on evidence. We follow the research and tell you honestly what it does and doesn't show.
