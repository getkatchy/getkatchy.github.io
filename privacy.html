Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

This page covers the waitlist on this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the waitlist form or the survey: your email address and, if you add them, your university and your survey answers. We also note which link brought you here (for example a WhatsApp group or TikTok), so we know which channels work. When you join you also get a personal invite link; if friends join through it, we can see how many people you brought (to reward the people who bring the most).

To count visits we use GoatCounter ↗, an open-source visit counter that sets no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. It sees which page was opened, the referring site, and your browser and country in broad terms, but not who you are. This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers.

Why

To email you about Katchy's launch and early access, and to understand whether students need Katchy (survey answers are only looked at as totals). We don't sell your email or share it with anyone for marketing.

How it's handled

When you submit the form, it's sent through a form service (FormSubmit) that delivers it to the Katchy team's inbox by email. We only keep it as long as the waitlist and early access need it.

Your choices

  • Leave the list any time by replying “stop” to any of our emails.
  • Ask us to see or delete your data by messaging us on LinkedIn ↗.

In the app

Katchy turns speech into text on your device. It never infers your emotions, and it never sends data about your attention to your university.
