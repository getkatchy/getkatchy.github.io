Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

This page covers the waitlist on this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the waitlist form: your email address and, if you add it, your university. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers.

Why

To email you about Katchy's launch and early access. That's it. We don't sell your email or share it with anyone for marketing.

How it's handled

When you submit the form, it's sent through a form service (FormSubmit) that delivers it to the Katchy team's inbox by email. We only keep it as long as the waitlist and early access need it.

Your choices

  • Leave the list any time by replying “stop” to any of our emails.
  • Ask us to see or delete your data by messaging us on LinkedIn ↗.

In the app

Katchy turns speech into text on your device. It never infers your emotions, and it never sends data about your attention to your university.
